Funnel Economics

Start tracking the numbers that actually make you money.

Metric What we care about Why it matters
Qualified registration flow & quality Are the right people entering the funnel? Cheap but unqualified leads do not create sustainable scale.
Registration ➔ show-up Are registrants actually attending? A full registration list is useless if the webinar room stays empty.
Show-up ➔ pitch retention Are people staying long enough to hear the offer? Low retention kills conversion before the offer is even judged.
Pitch ➔ purchase Does the webinar convert attention into students? This is where traffic becomes revenue.
CAC / ROAS Can you acquire a customer profitably? This is the number that determines whether scaling makes sense.

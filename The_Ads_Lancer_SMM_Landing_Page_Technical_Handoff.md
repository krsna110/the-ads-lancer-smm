# 1\. Core Positioning

Primary value proposition:

**We turn your knowledge, experience and personality into a strategic social media presence that builds authority, earns trust and supports qualified enquiries.**

The service should be presented as more than posting or content production. The underlying mechanism is:

Expertise → Strategy → Content → Authority → Trust → Business conversations

## Primary audience

- Coaches
- Consultants
- Astrologers / spiritual experts
- Personal brands and subject-matter experts

## Primary problem

Their expertise may be strong, but their social media presence is often inconsistent, random, difficult to manage, or insufficiently convincing to prospective clients.

## Primary promise

Build a professional, consistent and strategically managed digital presence without forcing the expert to manage the entire content operation themselves.

## Primary CTA

**CTA: APPLY FOR A STRATEGY CALL**

Use one dominant CTA across the page. Secondary actions should support the main CTA rather than introduce many different conversion goals.

**What exactly is included?**

Our social media management covers strategy, content planning, content creation, copywriting, publishing and performance analysis. The exact scope is finalized based on your brand, requirements and strategy.

Instagram is a primary focus for most expert-led personal brands. Depending on your audience and business model, we can also explore LinkedIn and other relevant platforms.

Yes. Reels, carousels, static posts and stories can all be incorporated into the content system based on what makes strategic sense for the brand.

Your involvement is primarily around strategy, expertise and approvals. The goal is to remove the day-to-day content workload from your calendar.

Content is planned and shared with you before publishing so you have the opportunity to review and approve it.

**Can you build my personal brand?**

Yes. Personal branding is part of the core strategy, including positioning, communication style, tone and visual identity.

**Do you work specifically with coaches and consultants?**

Yes. The service is designed primarily for expert-led businesses where the individual is an important part of the brand.

**Do you provide content strategy?**

Yes. Every engagement begins with strategy because content without direction can easily become noise.

**How long does it take to see improvement?**

There is no universal timeline because every account, audience, offer and market is different. The objective of the first phase is to establish the right positioning, content system, consistency and feedback loop.

**Do you guarantee followers or leads?**

No. We do not promise a fixed number of followers, enquiries or revenue. We build the strategy, content system and execution required to create stronger conditions for audience growth, trust and business conversations.

# 4\. Technical / Development Handoff

## Primary conversion goal

The page should drive qualified applications for a strategy call. Do not split attention between multiple unrelated primary conversions.

## CTA behavior

- Primary CTA label: APPLY FOR A STRATEGY CALL
- Every primary CTA should link to the same application form or same booking/application mechanism.
- Keep CTA language consistent across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Do not use "Submit", "Learn More" or "Get Started" as the primary CTA.

## Recommended application fields

| **Field**                                          | **Required?** | **Purpose**                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name                                               | Required      | Basic identification.                                       |
| Email                                              | Required      | Primary contact.                                            |
| Phone / WhatsApp                                   | Required      | Fast follow-up.                                             |
| Instagram / social profile URL                     | Required      | Allows pre-call review.                                     |
| Business / profession                              | Required      | Coach, consultant, astrologer, etc.                         |
| What do you currently sell?                        | Required      | Qualifies the business model.                               |
| Current monthly revenue / revenue range            | Optional      | Use only if the sales team needs this qualification signal. |
| What is your biggest social media challenge?       | Required      | Captures intent and sales context.                          |
| What do you want social media to help you achieve? | Required      | Clarifies desired business outcome.                         |
| Approximate monthly marketing budget               | Optional      | Use for qualification, not as a vanity metric.              |

## Form confirmation / next step

After submission, clearly tell the prospect what happens next. Example: "Thanks — your application has been received. We'll review your brand and contact you regarding the strategy call."

## Tracking requirements

- GA4 page_view and form_start/form_submit events.
- Meta Pixel / Conversions API where applicable, with correct event deduplication if both browser and server events are used.
- Track CTA clicks separately from successful form submissions.
- Track scroll depth at meaningful thresholds (25%, 50%, 75%, 90%) for CRO analysis.
- Track FAQ expansion clicks.
- Track outbound booking/WhatsApp clicks if used.
- Preserve UTM parameters into the lead/application record where technically possible.

## Performance / UX requirements

- Mobile-first implementation. Most social traffic is expected to be mobile.
- Do not use oversized hero artwork that pushes the actual value proposition below the fold.
- Use compressed WebP/AVIF images and lazy-load below-the-fold media.
- Keep the first meaningful content visually fast; avoid unnecessary animation before the CTA.
- Use sticky mobile CTA only if it does not obstruct content or form controls.
- Maintain accessible heading hierarchy (one H1, then logical H2/H3 levels).
- Make buttons large enough for mobile tapping and visually distinct from ordinary links.

## Proof / asset requirements before publish

- Replace any placeholder client logos or case-study cards with approved assets.
- Verify every numerical case-study claim before publishing.
- Get permission for client names, photographs and direct testimonials.
- Add screenshots or visual proof to case studies wherever confidentiality allows.
- Add founder/team photos if available to increase human trust.
- If monthly deliverables have fixed quantities, add those exact numbers to the offer section.

## Content items that should NOT be fabricated

- Follower growth numbers
- Lead or revenue numbers
- ROAS or ad-spend figures
- Client testimonials
- Waiting-list claims
- Pipeline values
- Guarantees or fixed future outcomes
- Certifications, credentials or partnerships

# 5\. CRO Notes for the Designer / Developer

- The hero should communicate audience + mechanism + business value in under 5 seconds of scanning.
- Visually separate strategy from execution so the service does not look like a commodity posting package.
- Use one consistent CTA style and label across the page.
- Place proof close to important claims; do not isolate all proof at the bottom.
- Use generous whitespace and premium typography; avoid a crowded agency-template look.
- Keep long text collapsed where appropriate on mobile, especially FAQs and detailed case-study copy.
- Do not over-animate the page. Motion should support hierarchy, not distract from the application CTA.
- Keep forms friction-aware: collect only information that will actually be used by the sales/strategy team.

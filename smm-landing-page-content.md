## 13. FAQ

### What exactly is included in social media management?

Everything from strategy and content planning to creative production, copywriting, publishing and performance analysis — the full breakdown is in the Services section above. The exact scope for your brand is confirmed on the strategy call.

### Which platforms do you manage?

Primarily Instagram, since that's where most coaches, consultants and experts build authority. We can extend to LinkedIn or other platforms depending on where your audience actually is.

### Do you also create reels?

Yes. Reels, carousels, static posts and stories are all part of the content system, planned around your strategy rather than created randomly.

### Do I need to provide videos/content?

Some raw material helps — talking-head clips, photos, or your expertise on a call or in writing — but our team handles the scripting, editing and turning it into finished content.

### How much involvement is required from me?

Enough to keep the content authentic — mainly your input during strategy and approval. We handle the day-to-day production and publishing.

### How does the approval process work?

Content is planned and shared with you ahead of publishing so you can review and approve it before anything goes live.

### Can you manage my personal brand?

Yes — personal branding is core to what we do, not an add-on. Positioning, tone and visual identity are built around you specifically.

### Do you work specifically with coaches and consultants?

We specialize in coaches, consultants, astrologers and other personal-brand-led experts — businesses where the person is the brand.

### Do you also help with content strategy?

Yes — every engagement starts with strategy. Content without a strategy behind it is just noise.
